1. The service

PostProd is a personal post-production tool operated by Clement Consulting. It runs on the computer of the video creator who uses it, to prepare videos and schedule them on that creator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

2. Accounts

Only accounts that belong to the person using PostProd may be connected, through each platform's official authorization page. An account can be disconnected at any time in PostProd, and access can be revoked from the platform itself.

3. Content

The creator is responsible for the videos, captions and settings they schedule, and for making sure they comply with the terms and community guidelines of each platform, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. PostProd claims no rights over any content.

4. How PostProd uses TikTok

  • By default, PostProd uploads a scheduled video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is public until the creator finishes and publishes the draft in the TikTok app.
  • Direct publishing is off by default. When turned on, PostProd publishes only the videos the creator explicitly scheduled, with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings the creator chose in the form, after the creator agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation (and Branded Content Policy when relevant).
  • PostProd uses TikTok's official APIs only and respects their limits. It never posts, comments, likes or follows on its own.

5. No warranty

PostProd is provided as is. Platforms may change or refuse a request, and a scheduled upload may fail or be delayed (for example while the creator's computer is off). Clement Consulting is not liable for a missed or delayed publication.
