PostProd (PP)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

PostProd is a personal post-production tool operated by Clement Consulting for one video creator, who uses it to schedule videos on their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what PostProd accesses through those platforms, why, and where it is kept.

1. Data PostProd accesses

PostProd only accesses accounts that their owner connects through the platform's official authorization page. From TikTok, with the permissions granted at connection:

  • user.info.basic: the account's open ID and display name, to show which account is connected;
  • video.list: the list of the account's videos with their view, like and comment counts and share link, to show the creator's own statistics;
  • video.upload: the right to upload a video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft, which the creator then finishes and publishes in the TikTok app;
  • video.publish: requested only if direct publishing is turned on (it is off by default), to publish a video the creator scheduled, with the caption and privacy settings the creator chose.

From YouTube and Instagram, PostProd similarly accesses the connected channel or professional account, uploads the videos the creator scheduled, and reads the statistics of those accounts' own videos.

PostProd does not access followers, messages, comments' contents, contacts or any data about other people.

2. How the data is used

Only to upload the creator's own videos at the time the creator chose, and to show the creator the statistics of their own videos (including which days and hours work best). PostProd does not use the data for advertising or profiling, does not sell it, does not share it with any third party, and does not use it to train any model.

3. Where the data is kept

  • PostProd runs on the creator's own computer. Schedules, statistics and account names are kept in a local database on that computer.
  • Access tokens are encrypted at rest with a key kept on that computer, and are never written to logs.
  • Video files stay on the creator's computer and are only sent to the platform the creator chose. For an Instagram Reel, Meta fetches the video through a temporary private link that expires and is deleted once the Reel is published.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until the creator disconnects the account in PostProd, which deletes them.
  • Statistics are kept locally to compute the best posting times; only the last 12 months are used. They are deleted with the local database.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform itself: in the TikTok app, under the settings that manage app permissions; in the Google Account's third-party access page; in Instagram's apps and websites settings.

5. Children

PostProd is not directed at children and is not offered to the public.
