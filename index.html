PostProd (PP)

A personal post-production tool for one video creator.

PostProd helps a single video creator prepare gaming videos and schedule them on the creator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It runs on the creator's own computer. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up and there are no other users.

What PostProd does with TikTok

  • Connect. The creator connects their own TikTok account through TikTok's official authorization page (Login Kit), and can disconnect it at any time.
  • Send drafts. At the date and time the creator chooses, PostProd uploads the video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API). The creator then opens the TikTok app, adds the caption and publishes it themselves.
  • Read statistics. PostProd reads the view, like and comment counts of the creator's own TikTok videos, to show the creator which days and hours work best for them.
PostProd only acts on accounts that belong to the creator, and only on the creator's own videos. It does not post, comment, like or follow anything on its own, and it never shares data with anyone else.

YouTube and Instagram

The same tool schedules the creator's videos on their own YouTube channel and Reels on their own Instagram professional account, through the official APIs of Google and Meta, and reads the statistics of those videos.
